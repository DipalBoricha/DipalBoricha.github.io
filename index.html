INTRODUCTION

About Me

Professional background, technical interests and approach to IT education.

IT Education & Practical Training

I am an IT educator and technical trainer with experience dating back to 2012. My core areas include computer hardware, networking, CCNA topics, server administration, Linux and cybersecurity.

From July 2017 to March 2025, I owned the IANT Computer Education franchise in Bhavnagar. I am now building AIT – Advanced Information Technology to support practical, lab-based learning and stronger hands-on technical skills.

My teaching style focuses on clear explanations, guided practice and troubleshooting. Cybersecurity is taught with an emphasis on authorization, safety and ethical use of tools.
